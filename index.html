    We look at the space, talk through colors and finishes, and give you a written estimate with every step explained.

About Made

Your estimate is our promise.

Every Made estimate lays out, step by step, what will be done, and often why each step matters. It isn't a rough guess. It's the commitment we make to you. What we agree on is what we deliver.

Made was founded to bring the standard we learned over more than 30 years of combined experience in professional painting straight to our clients.

Our specialty is residential interior painting. That's where we deliver our finest finish: walls, ceilings, kitchen cabinets, stairs, handrails and balusters.

We also take on commercial painting, from storefronts and offices to rental apartments, at the same standard, whether you own the property or you're the general contractor. And we paint exteriors too, residential and commercial, along with pressure washing.
